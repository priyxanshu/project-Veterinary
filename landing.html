About the council

A statutory register for veterinary practice in Madhya Pradesh

The Madhya Pradesh State Veterinary Council maintains the official register of qualified veterinary practitioners in the state, constituted under the Indian Veterinary Council Act, 1984. It regulates registration, professional conduct and continuing standards for the profession.

The Council is composed of elected, nominated and ex-officio members, supported by sub-committees overseeing ethics, education and grievance redressal — the same structure that reviews every application filed through this portal.

Common questions

Frequently asked

How long does new registration take to process?

Most complete applications are verified and approved within 4–7 working days of submission. You can track progress from your dashboard at any time.

What documents do I need for renewal?

Your existing certificate number, a recent passport-size photograph, and proof of continued practice or updated address if it has changed.

Can I transfer my registration from another state council?

Yes — apply online under Transfer Registration along with the No-Objection Certificate issued by your originating state council.

How do I track a complaint I've filed?

Open Complaint Management → Status and enter your complaint reference number to see real-time updates on review.

I lost my certificate — how do I get a duplicate?

Apply for a Duplicate Certificate online against your registration number; an affidavit or police report copy may be requested for lost certificates.
